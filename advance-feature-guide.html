SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

From Price Watching to Mathematical Strike Filtration

Retail traders get overwhelmed comparing 40+ strikes across call and put columns. The Alpha Edge Advance Feature V2.1 algorithm condenses market depth into four automated components running directly inside your TradingView layout.

PILLAR 01 · AUTOMATION

Buyer / Seller Active Strikes Board

Instantly aggregates all shortlisted CE and PE contracts across your active index or stock. It checks whether the session low or high tested the institutional absorption zone and displays only actionable candidates.

Fields: Signal · Type · Strike · LTP · Open · High · Low
PILLAR 02 · MATRIX

CE & PE Session Extreme Tracker

Tracks the session Low and High for both call and put sides simultaneously. Compares Call Low against the O–P band (Optimal to Participation) and Put High against the U–O band, highlighting anomalies before spot breaks out.

Sync: Live NSE Spot + 12 Option Strikes
PILLAR 03 · EXECUTION

Trend Confirmation Line (TCL)

A non-lagging, premium-weighted dynamic line plotted per option strike. If a call premium trades above TCL, the buyer setup is validated; if premium falls below TCL, the trade is automatically invalidated.

Logic: Premium > TCL = Active Trend Flow
PILLAR 04 · ANCHORING

Dynamic ATM Reference Anchor

Continuously identifies the exact At-The-Money strike relative to live spot movement (e.g., NIFTY at ₹24,814.50 automatically marks 24,800 as ATM), preventing traders from mistakenly trading illiquid far-OTM strikes.

Step Size: 50 pts (Nifty) / 100 pts (Bank Nifty)

EXECUTION PROTOCOL

Five-Step Rule for Trade Execution

Never execute based on intuition. The Advance Feature enforces a strict sequential gate before taking any position.

Check Spot Index Trend

Identify whether NIFTY 50 / BANK NIFTY is sustaining above the day's median VWAP or rejecting key institutional levels.

Read U.O.P. Envelope

Confirm where option premiums sit relative to Participation (P), Optimal (O), and Universal (U) structural thresholds.

Check Low vs O–P Band

Verify that the contract's session low held inside the institutional accumulation band (between Optimal and Participation).

Inspect Filtration Board

The candidate contract must display green ACTIVE status on the board with Signal matching your index bias.

Confirm Above TCL

Enter only when option premium sustains above its dynamic TCL. Invalidation is fixed below TCL with predefined ₹ risk.

Scale into Targets

Lock profits at Side Up 1 (SU1) and trail stop-loss to breakeven, allowing runners to reach Main Target 1 (MT1).
