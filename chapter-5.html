How to Find CE & PE with the AlphaEdgePro Indicator

U.O.P Theory Bootcamp · Lesson 5 of 12

What you will learn

Bring the CE and PE premium details onto one screen, shortlist strikes using the U.O.P framework, and read the filtration result before reviewing a setup.

  1. Read Universal, Optimal and Participation on the chart.
  2. Compare CE and PE High, Low, LTP, TCL and status by strike.
  3. Check the main filtration board for the final displayed candidates.
From a single price to a complete premium view

You can now see more than the last traded price.

Until now, a key challenge has been checking the High and Low of CE and PE premiums together while selecting a strike. AlphaEdgePro brings the solution into your chart workspace: Open, High, Low and LTP can be read alongside the U.O.P framework. In the supplied layout, the option chain displays High, Low and LTP for both sides, while the main filtration board includes an Open column for shortlisted contracts.

Instead of judging an option from its current price alone, compare where its premium has travelled, where it stands relative to U.O.P, and what the indicator’s filtration board is showing.

01

U.O.P Section

Your premium reference levels

This section places the three core U.O.P numbers on the selected option chart. They provide the reference points used to compare the premium’s High, Low and current price.

LevelScreenshot valueHow to read it
Participation (P)207.00 · GreenThe upper reference in the O-to-P buyer-candidate band.
Optimal (O)179.00 · YellowThe middle reference separating the U-to-O and O-to-P bands.

Under the course’s screening rules, a session Low between O and P identifies a buyer-side candidate to examine. A session High between U and O identifies a seller-side candidate to examine. Continue with price and TCL confirmation; a band match is the first filter.

03

Main Filtration Board

The displayed buyer / seller shortlist

The lower-right board is labelled ALPHAEDGEPRO | BUYER / SELLER ACTIVE STRIKES. It summarises the indicator’s filtered output using Signal, Type, Strike, LTP, Open, High and Low, so you can review the selected contract and its premium details together.

What this screenshot actually shows

The board reads NO ACTIVE STRIKES, with BUY 0 | SELL 0 and 0/0 shown. The chain’s ACTIVE label therefore does not establish a qualified entry on this board at the captured moment. Read the chain filter and the final board as separate stages of the process.

When the board displays a candidate, match its Type and Strike to the correct CE or PE chart. Use its Open, High, Low and LTP to review the contract, then check the live price and TCL conditions taught in the earlier chapters.

The practical sequence

How to find and review a CE or PE candidate

  1. Confirm the contract. Check NIFTY, the selected expiry, strike and CE/PE type. The supplied chart is a one-minute 22800 PE chart; use the appropriate settings for your session.
  2. Read the U.O.P section. Note U, O and P before checking the chain.
  3. Scan both premium sides. Compare each Low with O–P and each High with U–O. Note any highlighted or ACTIVE rows.
  4. Check the main filtration board. Read the actual displayed output. If it shows no active strikes, wait for a qualifying board result rather than treating the chain label as an entry.
  5. Review the candidate chart. Match the strike and expiry, compare price with TCL and the relevant U.O.P level, and write down the setup’s invalidation before considering a trade.

Chapter 5 takeaway: U.O.P gives the reference levels. The option chain shows the CE/PE premium details. The main filtration board shows the filtered shortlist. Read all three together: levels → chain → board → chart confirmation.
