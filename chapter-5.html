ITM Strike Selection & Gap Scenarios

U.O.P Theory Bootcamp · Lesson 5 of 10

What you will learn

Use ITM closing premiums to form candidate NIFTY zones, then combine strike selection with live premium-zone checks.

  1. Scan ITM CE and PE rows outward from spot.
  2. Find the first and second closes above Participation.
  3. Mark candidate spot zones, then verify both premium maps and TCL.
Chapter 5 · Strike Selection

Find candidate NIFTY support and resistance from ITM CE and PE premiums.

The U.O.P method compares each in-the-money option’s closing premium with the Participation number. Scan the ITM CE strikes below NIFTY spot for support candidates, and the ITM PE strikes above spot for resistance candidates. Then examine the selected options’ own premium zones and TCL before deciding whether either side is still active.

Why these are support candidates

Starting with the nearest ITM CE and moving down the strikes, 24100 is the first closing premium above P. The next qualifying strike is 24050, shown as UP2. The method therefore watches 24100–24050 as a NIFTY support area and selects 24050 CE as its stronger ITM CE reference. Observe whether spot and premium actually respond there.

Why these are resistance candidates

Starting with the nearest ITM PE and moving up, 24350 is the first closing premium above P. At 24400, the PE premium is also above P and marked UP2. The method watches 24350–24400 as a resistance area and selects 24400 PE as its stronger ITM PE reference. A touch alone cannot prove rejection.

The second decision · premium-zone status

Gap-up and gap-down: read both premium maps.

The supplied visual illustrates a gap-up. The matching gap-down visual reverses the illustrative premium-zone roles: PE in the U.O.P “Deep ITM” main-upside region and CE in the U.O.P “OTM” main-downside region. These are conditional examples, not an automatic result of every gap. Standard ITM status still depends on current spot versus strike; recheck both selected strikes after the gap. The historical table above only shows UP2 labels and does not establish either live zone state.

OTM-zone strength test: If a selected option premium reaches the U.O.P downside main-target region yet Main Down TG 3 holds with TCL support, it may still rebound. If premium breaks Main Down TG 3 and TCL confirms the downside, the hold-and-recovery thesis needs reassessment. Define invalidation and risk before considering a trade.

Chapter 5 takeaway: Compare the ITM CE and PE closing premiums to Participation, step outward to find the first and second qualifying strikes, and mark their candidate NIFTY support and resistance zones. On a gap-up, look for a CE upside-zone / PE downside-zone split; on a gap-down, look for the reverse. Confirm the actual live zones, TCL, and each strike’s current moneyness. The illustrated 24100–24050 support and 24350–24400 resistance are historical method outputs, not permanent barriers.
