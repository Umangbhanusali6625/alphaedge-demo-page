CHAPTER 7 · TRADE MANAGEMENT

Main Targets & Rejection

U.O.P Theory Bootcamp · Lesson 8 of 10

What you will learn

Distinguish a confirmed continuation from a rejection at Main Up TG 1, TG 2 or TG 3.

  1. Confirm a sustained move above Side Up 3 with TCL.
  2. Reassess strength at each main target.
  3. Identify continuation or rejection and document the exit plan.
Chapter 7 · The Main Upside Move

After Morning Breakfast: continuation or rejection?

The first move in Chapter 6 reaches the Side Up levels. In this U.O.P example, a move above Side Up 3 (250.14) together with TCL trend confirmation opens a second roadmap toward the Main Upside targets. A brief spike alone does not confirm it.

1. Rejection from Main Up TG 1 · 291.00

After the Side Up 3 breakout and TCL confirmation, premium reaches the first main target. If it cannot hold above 291.00 and turns down, watch for a pullback toward TCL. The second target has not opened until price confirms continuation above TG 1.

2. Rejection from Main Up TG 2 · 341.48

Premium holds beyond TG 1 and reaches TG 2. This is generally the method’s full profit-booking zone for a lower-risk CE buyer. If price then fails to sustain above 341.48, a pullback can follow. Observe TCL and the actual price response before considering any separate contra plan.

3. Rejection from Main Up TG 3 · 395.76

Only a continued move through TG 2 brings the extended TG 3 into focus. Price may touch 395.76 and turn lower. At this point, reassess whether the premium holds the target and whether TCL supports the move; an extended target can still reject.

Above Side Up 3 · 250.14→TCL confirms→Main TG 1 · 291.00→Main TG 2 · 341.48→Main TG 3 · 395.76

When does the second move remain open?

After price clears Side Up 3, check that the TCL supports the direction and that premium can sustain the move. Main TG 1 is the first reference. If strength and TCL confirmation continue beyond it, Main TG 2 becomes the next reference. Main TG 3 at 395.76 is assessed only after another confirmed continuation beyond TG 2.

A target is a place to reassess price, not an instruction to hold until it is reached.

Where can rejection begin?

  • Main TG 1: a touch or wick near 291.00 without sustained TCL confirmation can reject. Treat it as a failed continuation until the price proves otherwise.
  • Main TG 2: near 341.48 is the method's full profit-booking zone for a low-risk trader. A pullback may start here, so protect gains and observe the response.
  • Main TG 3: near 395.76 is the extended target. Even after a strong move through TG 2, premium can reject here; reassess TCL, price acceptance, and exit risk.
Contra and scalper observation

A rejection is a setup to verify, not a trade by itself.

Suppose the morning active option is a CE and its premium reaches Main TG 2. If it touches the zone, fails to hold above it, and TCL or price action turns against the move, the CE may pull back. The same confirmation principle applies at TG 1 and TG 3. A verified rejection at any target can prompt a separate short-duration contra or scalper plan. Record the reversal confirmation, entry, invalidation, and exit; a target touch alone is insufficient.

Day filter: This continuation and rejection discussion is for the method's Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday sessions. On Monday and Tuesday, option premiums can be compressed near expiry and may break out or break down sharply; use the separate extension and expiry rules from Chapter 6.1. Compression does not predict direction or make selling premium automatically safe.

Chapter 7 takeaway: Side Up 3 plus TCL confirmation is the gateway to Main TG 1, then Main TG 2. Each target can reject: TG 1 at 291.00 if TCL fails, TG 2 at 341.48 as the method’s profit-booking and reversal-watch zone, and TG 3 at 395.76 after an extended move. Contra or scalper action needs its own evidence and risk plan.

PRACTICE CHECKPOINT

Explain it before moving on.

Compare the three rejection charts. For each, explain what shows failed continuation and what additional evidence would be needed for a separate contra plan.

Review the chart, explain the conditions in your own words, and record what would invalidate the idea.
